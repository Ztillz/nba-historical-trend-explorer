Data and interpretation

Read with context

Ratios use summed numerators and denominators. Efficiencies use NBA possessions; rebound shares use box-score opportunities and can differ from NBA’s advanced rebound percentages. True shooting uses the 0.44 free-throw approximation. “Change” is the second half minus the first half of the observation window.

These are exploratory retrospective comparisons. Several questions and horizons share samples; intervals are not adjusted for multiple testing. Opponent strength, injuries, roster changes, and repeated-franchise dependence remain limitations.

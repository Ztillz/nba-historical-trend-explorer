## Interpretation

Correlation screens relationships; the later-season benchmark asks whether the extra signals improve a simple linear baseline. A strong raw correlation does not establish incremental usefulness. Later seasons have already been inspected during this project, so this split is a retrospective evaluation, not an untouched prospective test.

Intervals resample entire seasons, preserving within-season dependence; repeated franchises across seasons remain a limitation. No multiple-testing-adjusted significance claims are made. Point differential is points per game, not possession-adjusted net rating.
